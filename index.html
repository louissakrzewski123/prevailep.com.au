The basics

What is an Accredited Exercise Physiologist?

An AEP is a university-qualified allied health professional who uses movement and exercise to prevent and manage injury, disability and chronic conditions. We're recognised by Medicare, the NDIS, DVA and private health funds.

Think of an AEP as the bridge between the clinic and everyday life — translating your goals into safe, achievable movement that fits your body, your condition and your world. Not a gym program. A plan built for you.

Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do I need a referral?

Not for NDIS or private sessions — you can reach out directly. For a Medicare CDM plan you'll need a referral from your GP, and DVA requires a referral (D904).

What areas do you cover?

In-home and community sessions across Brisbane and South-East Queensland, plus telehealth for clients anywhere in Australia.

Do I need to be fit or sporty?

Not at all. Programs start exactly where you are — many clients are completely new to structured exercise, and that's welcome.

What conditions do you work with?

A wide range, including neurological and developmental conditions, brain injury, chronic pain and disease, and mental health. If you're unsure whether I can help, just ask.

How much does it cost?

NDIS sessions are charged within the current NDIS price limits. Private, Medicare and DVA fees are provided on enquiry — I'll always be upfront about costs before we begin.

Why is there a waitlist?

As a solo practice I keep numbers low to protect the quality of care. The waitlist lets me onboard new clients thoughtfully. Join it and I'll be in touch as soon as a spot opens.
