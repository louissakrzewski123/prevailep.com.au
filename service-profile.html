Prevail is a solo Accredited Exercise Physiology practice supporting people living with disability, injury and complex health needs to build strength, confidence and independence — delivered in the participant's own home, in their community, or via telehealth. Caseload is kept deliberately small so every participant sees the same clinician every session.

1 dayReferrals acknowledged
2 weeksTo initial assessment
5 daysReports delivered
1 clinicianEvery session

Services

  • Comprehensive assessment with baseline outcome measures
  • Individualised exercise programs for the home environment
  • In-home and community-based sessions
  • Telehealth consultations Australia-wide
  • Functional capacity and mobility work
  • Movement-based support for mental health and regulation
  • Written progress reporting for plan reviews

Who I work with

  • Cerebral palsy and other neurodevelopmental conditions
  • Acquired and traumatic brain injury
  • Epilepsy
  • Autism and intellectual disability
  • Neurodegenerative conditions
  • Chronic pain, fatigue and chronic disease
  • Co-occurring mental health challenges

Funding accepted

  • NDIS — self-managed and plan-managed participants
  • Medicare — Chronic Disease Management (GP referral)
  • DVA — Gold and White Card holders (D904 referral)
  • WorkCover — approved claims
  • Private — no referral required

Not NDIS-registered — NDIA-managed funding cannot be used.

NDIS delivery

Delivered under Capacity Building — Improved Daily Living.

All charges within the current NDIS Pricing Arrangements. Travel and agreed report-writing time itemised on every invoice.

Plan-managed participants: invoiced directly to the plan manager. Self-managed: invoiced to the participant or nominee, fortnightly.

Coverage

In-home and community sessions across Brisbane — north, south, east and west — plus Logan, Redlands, Ipswich and Moreton Bay.

Telehealth available to participants anywhere in Australia.

Credentials

  • ESSA-Accredited Exercise Physiologist (AEP)
  • University-qualified, evidence-based practice
  • Full professional indemnity and public liability insurance
